# APX RIDE Data Protection Checklist

## Before live customer use

- [ ] Confirm the business legal name, address and privacy contact.
- [ ] Choose and document the lawful basis for bookings, dispatch, complaints, lost property, driver records and accounting.
- [ ] Publish a customer privacy notice before collecting personal information.
- [ ] Confirm whether the business must pay the ICO data protection fee.
- [ ] Restrict the deployed portal to named authorised users and remove test users who no longer need access.
- [ ] Use unique user accounts and enable multi-factor authentication on every account where available.
- [ ] Record which staff may view, change, export or message customer data.
- [ ] Agree and document retention periods; configure a regular review process.
- [ ] Store exported backups encrypted, test restoration and document results.
- [ ] Put written processor agreements in place for every supplier handling personal information.
- [ ] Create a subject access request process and identity-verification checklist.
- [ ] Create an erasure and correction process that respects legal retention obligations.
- [ ] Create a data-breach plan with named decision makers and contact details.
- [ ] Train every authorised user and retain training evidence.

## Data minimisation rules

- Record only contact and journey information needed to quote, book, dispatch, account for and handle complaints about the service.
- Do not place payment-card details, health information, passport data or criminal-record documents in booking notes.
- Keep licence and DBS document references in the compliance register; store source documents only in an approved restricted document repository.
- Do not reuse passenger phone numbers for marketing without a separate lawful basis and the required electronic-marketing compliance.
