Useful answers

Before you request a ride.

When will I receive the fare?

APX RIDE will review your request and email a fare. You can reply to agree it or discuss your plans before any booking is confirmed.

How many passengers and bags can travel?

The MPV has up to seven passenger seats and space for up to four large suitcases plus four small bags. Bag sizes vary, so include accurate luggage details in your request.

Does submitting the form confirm my ride?

No. Your journey is confirmed only when APX RIDE accepts the request and sends a booking confirmation.

Can I request a trip outside Gatwick, Reigate or Redhill?

Yes. Add the full pickup and destination details and APX RIDE can review the route.
